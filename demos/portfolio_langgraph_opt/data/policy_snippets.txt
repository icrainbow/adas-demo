Avoid recommending illiquid assets when client has high liquidity needs or short time horizon.
Do not recommend high-volatility products to clients with stated low risk appetite.
Flag portfolios where single position exceeds 25% or single sector exceeds 40% of total holdings.
Consider client age and time to retirement when evaluating exposure to high-volatility assets.
Complex or leveraged products require documented evidence of client experience and understanding.
Leverage products must include clear disclosure of downside amplification and liquidation risks.
Clients over 65 with aggressive growth requests require time horizon and recovery capacity review.
Any suitability concern must be explained in plain language the client can understand.
Concentration risk increases when adding to existing large positions; assess diversification impact.
Escalate to human review when client profile conflicts with requested action on multiple dimensions.
Match portfolio liquidity profile to client stated liquidity needs within 12-month planning horizon.
Inexperienced clients requesting complex derivatives or structured products require additional due diligence.
